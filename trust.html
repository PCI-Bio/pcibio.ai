Safety & data

Built to carry a pathologist's signature.

The software says when a slide can't be read reliably, the pathologist signs every result, and patient data stays out of the system.

Built-in safeguards

When a slide can't be read reliably, it says so.

Instead of a confident wrong answer, the case gets no call and one plain sentence explaining why. The grades stay available to the lab for review.

Control lines

The controls didn't stain

If the three control lines on the slide did not stain as expected, there is no scale to grade against.

The case reads: No call. The on-slide control lines did not stain enough to grade the tissue.
Tumor

Too little tumor

A call needs enough tumor nuclei. A small or sparse sample is flagged instead of guessed.

The case reads: No call. Too few tumor nuclei to grade.
Slides

The two slides don't line up

When the tumor region comes from the H&E and the two slides can't be matched, the read stops there.

The case reads: No call. The H&E and p53 slides could not be aligned.
Before signing

One deliberate step to sign

Signing asks the pathologist to confirm they looked at the slides and read the report. Only pathologists can sign.

The dialog reads: I looked at the slides and read this report.
After signing

Signed means final

A signed report is locked. Areas, notes and the summary can't be changed afterwards.

The case reads: Signed by the pathologist. The report is locked.
Always

Every step on record

Who marked the controls, who marked the tumor, who edited the summary and who signed, with the date and time.

The report reads: Control lines and tumor region marked by the laboratory and the pathologist.
Data & security

Patient data stays out. Lab data stays separate.

Labels removed at uploadThe slide label and overview photos are stripped from each slide file when it is uploaded.
No patient identity neededThe online requisition asks for the client's case code, not a name or date of birth.
Encrypted analysisSlides are analysed in an encrypted cloud environment, and all traffic to the platform uses HTTPS.
RolesHistotechnologists, pathologists and administrators each see and do only what their role needs.
Two-step sign-inAn authenticator app on top of the password, with one-time backup codes.
Audit trailEvery view, change and signature is logged in a record that can't be edited after the fact.

Each laboratory sees only its own cases. Research use only — not for use in diagnostic procedures.

Scoring criteria

The call follows published p53 criteria.

The software reads every case against the cell-line control panel on the slide (Hussain 2019), scores it against established, published p53 criteria (Köbel 2016; Yemelyanova 2011) and reports the pattern: overexpression, wild-type, null, or borderline for the pathologist's review. The same references are printed at the bottom of every report.
